* Fixed unreliable widget unlock/hide: the previous version deferred the
  actual unlock to a background job that Android doesn't guarantee runs
  promptly (battery optimization can delay it by seconds or more), so the
  widget often kept showing "locked" well after you'd already authenticated,
  making repeated taps behave inconsistently. The unlock now completes
  before the invisible prompt screen closes, so the widget reliably shows
  the correct state right away
