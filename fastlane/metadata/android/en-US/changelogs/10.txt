* Test & save: connection is now verified before overwriting your saved settings
* Cached login token and base currency cut background refreshes down to fewer requests
* Configurable refresh interval (15 min / 30 min / 1 h / 6 h)
* Each widget can now pin its own time range independently of the others
* Widget follows light/dark system theme instead of always being dark
* Numbers and percentages now format according to the chosen app language
* Stale data is now labelled with "yesterday" or the date instead of a misleading time
* http:// is now only allowed for local-network addresses; public servers require https://
* Background refresh no longer retries endlessly on configuration errors (e.g. wrong token)
