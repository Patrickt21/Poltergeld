* Fixed a real bug behind the widget's privacy lock: unlocking it could
  bring the whole app to the foreground instead of just revealing the
  amounts in place, because the invisible unlock screen shared a task with
  the app and Android merged them. It now runs in its own isolated task and
  can no longer surface the app by accident
* Fixed: rapidly tapping the widget's lock icon twice could show two
  overlapping fingerprint/PIN prompts at once
* Privacy Mode (widget): how long revealed amounts stay visible before
  auto-hiding again is now a setting (15s to 5 minutes) instead of a fixed
  60 seconds
* Removed the separate "hide again immediately" tap on the widget's lock
  icon while revealed – amounts now always auto-hide after the configured
  duration, one fewer tap target in a spot that has repeatedly had
  reliability trouble
