* Fixed: unlocking the widget's privacy mode sometimes appeared to do
  nothing – amounts stayed masked even after successful authentication.
  The widget froze its masked/unmasked decision for as long as its render
  session stayed alive, so an unlock only took effect once that session
  had timed out. The mask state is now observed live from inside the
  widget and reacts to every change immediately
* Fixed the mirror image of the same bug: amounts could stay *visible*
  past the configured reveal duration when the auto re-mask hit a still
  live render session
* Fixed a race where unlocking right as a previous reveal window expired
  could re-mask the amounts again immediately after authentication
